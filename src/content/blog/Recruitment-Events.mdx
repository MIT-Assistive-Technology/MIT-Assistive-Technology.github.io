## Find Us at the MIT Fall Activities Midway!

**Friday, September 4th, 2026**
**2:30 PM - 4:00 PM**
**Johnson Atletics Center (Building W34)**

AT @ MIT works with codesigners from the Greater Boston Area to address real problems they face in day to day life. Combining the expertise of our students and co-designers, we iteratively design and test innovative solutions. Come meet some current exec members and learn more!

## Event Details

- **When:** Friday, September 4th, 2026, 2:30 PM - 4:00 PM
- **Where:** Johnson Atletics Center (Building W34)
- **Who:** Open to the all MIT students
