<h1>
	<strong>Privacy Policy for CurbToCar</strong>
</h1>
<p>
	<strong>Effective Date</strong>: 10/28/2026
	<br />
	The MIT Assistive Technology Club is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of
	your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect
	your personal data when you use our mobile app, CurbToCar, and its services.
</p>
<p>
	CurbToCar is an assistive technology application designed to help blind and visually impaired
	users locate their vehicles. The app uses camera data, on-device object detection, compass data,
	and depth information to provide navigational assistance.
</p>
<h3>
	<strong>Data We Collect</strong>
</h3>
<p>When you use our mobile app, we collect the following types of personal information:</p>
<ol>
	<li>
		<strong>Vehicle Information</strong>
		<br />
		We collect information that you provide about your vehicle, including make, model, color, and
		license plate number.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: When you input "Blue Honda Civic, license ABC123", we store this information
			to help you locate your specific vehicle.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Camera Data</strong>
		<br />
		With your explicit consent, we access your device's camera to capture images of your
		surroundings to identify vehicles.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: When you scan your environment, the app captures images to detect and
			identify vehicles that may match your description.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Sensor Data</strong>
		<br />
		We collect data from your device's compass and depth sensors to provide navigational assistance.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: We use compass data to give you directional guidance like "Your car is 20
			feet ahead to your right."
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Device Information</strong>
		<br />
		We gather technical data about your mobile device, such as device type, operating system, and
		app version.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: We collect your device type (iPhone, Android) to ensure the app is
			compatible and runs smoothly on your device.
		</p>
		We also generate a random identifier when you install the app. It is not linked to your name,
		account, or contacts. It is used to protect the service from abuse and to count how many devices
		use it.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>IP Address</strong>
		<br />
		When using our services, your IP address may be collected and shared with our third-party
		service providers.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: Your IP address may be logged when making API calls to our vehicle
			recognition service.
		</p>
	</li>
</ol>
<h3>
	<strong>How We Use Your Information</strong>
</h3>
<p>We use the data we collect for the following specific purposes:</p>
<ol>
	<li>
		<strong>Vehicle Identification and Navigation</strong>
		<br />
		We use your vehicle information (make, model, color, license plate) and camera data to identify
		your vehicle and provide navigational guidance.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: When you scan your surroundings, we match captured images against your
			vehicle description to help you locate your car.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Third-Party Processing</strong>
		<br />
		We share images captured by your camera with Eydea Recognition (an external company) for vehicle
		make, model, and color recognition.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: When you scan the parking lot, images are sent to Eydea Recognition to
			identify vehicles in the frame.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>AI-Powered Matching</strong>
		<br />
		We share cropped images of detected vehicles, API results from Eydea Recognition, and your
		vehicle description with OpenAI to match detected vehicles with your specific vehicle.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: OpenAI helps determine if the "blue sedan" in a cropped image of a detected
			vehicle matches your "Blue Honda Civic" description.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Service Improvement</strong>
		<br />
		We analyze usage patterns and app performance to improve functionality and user experience.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: We may analyze how often the app successfully identifies vehicles to improve
			our detection algorithms.
		</p>
		To keep the service reliable, our servers record basic information about each request the app
		makes: which service was called, whether it succeeded, how long it took, and its size. We do not
		record the content of requests or your IP address in these metrics. To count active users
		without tracking them, we store only a one-way code derived from the random app-install
		identifier. The code changes every day, so activity cannot be linked across days. These metrics
		are kept for about three months. They are not used for advertising or tracking.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Technical Support</strong>
		<br />
		We use device information to troubleshoot issues and provide technical support.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: If you report a problem, we may use your device information to diagnose
			compatibility issues.
		</p>
	</li>
</ol>
<h3>
	<strong>Legal Basis for Processing</strong>
</h3>
<p>We process your data based on the following legal grounds:</p>
<ol>
	<li>
		<strong>Consent</strong>
		<br />
		We process your personal information when you have given us explicit consent, such as allowing
		the app to use your camera.
		<br />
		<em>Example</em>: You can withdraw your consent at any time by changing your device settings.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Performance of a Contract</strong>
		<br />
		We process your data to fulfill the terms of service you agree to when using our app. CurbToCar
		is provided free of charge, with no payment required.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: When you use CurbToCar, we process your vehicle information and camera data
			to provide the vehicle identification service as described in our terms of service.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Legitimate Interests</strong>
		<br />
		We may process your data for purposes aligned with our legitimate business interests, such as
		improving our app or conducting user analytics.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: Analyzing app usage data helps us improve the user experience and develop
			new features.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Compliance with Legal Obligations</strong>
		<br />
		We may process your data to comply with legal requirements or respond to legal requests when
		required.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: We may need to retain certain usage data for compliance with accessibility
			laws or respond to valid legal requests from authorities.
		</p>
	</li>
</ol>
<h3>
	<strong>Data Transfer Outside the EU</strong>
</h3>
<p>
	In some cases, we may need to transfer your personal data to countries outside the European Union
	(EU) or the European Economic Area (EEA). These transfers may occur when our service providers or
	partners are located in countries outside of the EU/EEA or when we need to store or process data
	in global data centers. We ensure that any such transfer of your personal data is carried out in
	compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation
	(GDPR). To safeguard your data during these transfers, we rely on standard contractual clauses or
	other appropriate safeguards, ensuring that your data is protected in accordance with GDPR
	standards.
</p>
<p>
	<strong>Use of Trackers</strong>
	<br />
	CurbToCar does not show advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or
	websites.
</p>

<ol>
	<li>

		<br />
		You can request access to the personal information we hold about you.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: You can ask for a copy of the data we have collected, such as your vehicle

		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Rectification</strong>
		<br />
		You have the right to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data we hold about you.

		<strong>Erasure</strong>
		<br />
		You can request the deletion of your data under certain circumstances.
		<p>

			erase any data we can link to you, unless we are required to retain it for legal reasons.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Restriction of Processing</strong>
		<br />
		You can request that we limit how we process your data in certain situations.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: You can request that we stop processing your data for marketing purposes.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Objection to Processing</strong>
		<br />
		You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data, especially for marketing
		purposes.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: You can opt out of receiving marketing emails or notifications by updating
			your app settings.
		</p>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Data Portability</strong>
		<br />
		You can request a copy of your data in a format that allows you to transfer it to another
		service.
		<p>
			<em>Example</em>: You can download a copy of your data to transfer it to another app if you
			choose to switch services.
		</p>
	</li>
</ol>
<h3>
	<strong>Data Security</strong>
</h3>
<p>
	We implement a range of technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from
	unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
</p>
<ol>
	<li>
		<strong>Encryption</strong>
		<p>
			We use encryption to protect data during transmission between your device and our servers.
		</p>
		<em>Example</em>: Images captured by your camera are encrypted during transmission to our
		vehicle recognition service.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Image Processing</strong>
		<p>
			Images captured by your camera are processed for vehicle identification and are not stored by
			us. Our third-party providers handle the images they receive under their own policies, linked
			below.
		</p>
		<em>Example</em>: After processing your images to identify vehicles, the images are not retained
		in our systems.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Access Controls</strong>
		<p>
			We limit access to your personal data to only those team members who need it to perform their
			job functions.
		</p>
		<em>Example</em>: Only developers working on improving vehicle recognition have access to
		anonymized usage data.
	</li>
</ol>
<h3>
	<strong>Data Retention</strong>
</h3>
<p>
	We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was
	collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
</p>
<p>
	<em>Example</em>: Request metrics are kept for about three months. Images are not stored by us.
</p>
<h3>
	<strong>Changes to This Policy</strong>
</h3>
<p>
	We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be posted within
	the app or on our website, and we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of the policy.
</p>
<h3>
	<strong>Third-Party Data Processing</strong>
</h3>
<p>Our app relies on the following third-party services to function properly:</p>
<ol>
	<li>
		<strong>Eydea Recognition</strong>
		<p>
			We share images captured by your camera with Eydea Recognition for vehicle make, model, and
			color recognition.
		</p>
		<em>Important</em>: Eydea Recognition may store your IP address and request data, but does not
		store or train on the images you submit. Their processing is used solely for the purpose of
		identifying vehicles in your images.
		<br />
		You can learn more about how Eydea Recognition processes your data by reviewing their{' '}
		<a href='https://store.eyedea.cz/privacy-policy/' target='_blank' rel='noopener noreferrer'>
			Privacy Policy
		</a>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>OpenAI</strong>
		<p>
			We share cropped images of detected vehicles, API results from Eydea Recognition, and your
			vehicle description with OpenAI to match detected vehicles with your specific vehicle.
		</p>
		<em>Important</em>: The data shared with OpenAI is limited to cropped images of detected
		vehicles (not full camera frames), detection results, and your vehicle description. This data is
		used solely for the purpose of matching detected vehicles to your description. OpenAI may keep
		API inputs for a limited period under its own policies. You can learn more about how OpenAI
		processes your data by reviewing their{' '}
		<a href='https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy' target='_blank' rel='noopener noreferrer'>
			Privacy Policy
		</a>
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Cloudflare</strong>
		<p>
			Requests from the app to Eydea Recognition and OpenAI pass through a relay we operate on
			Cloudflare's network. The relay forwards each request and does not store images or request
			content.
		</p>
		<em>Important</em>: As the host of the relay, Cloudflare processes the IP address of each
		request. You can learn more about how Cloudflare processes your data by reviewing their{' '}
		<a href='https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/' target='_blank' rel='noopener noreferrer'>
			Privacy Policy
		</a>
	</li>
</ol>
